What is this totem?

You have found one of the renegade beacons guiding walkers through the Midnight Renegade Art Walk. Spirits from forgotten galleries have slipped through a fissure in the park. Each beacon hides clues to their whereabouts and feeds a volumetric sculpture — the Rift Gate — with the emotions of the crowd.

  • TOUCH the beacon gently or scan the code to report what you discovered. This updates the shared story log for all walkers.
  • LISTEN for prompts. When a spirit is returned to the Rift Gate an encounter may begin. Vanquishing them restores balance to the installation.
  • RESPECT the site. Leave the totem exactly where you found it so the next artist can continue the game.

If you stumbled here by accident, consider it an invitation. Join the midnight procession, or simply walk away and keep our secret safe.
